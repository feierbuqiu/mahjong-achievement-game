# Code of conduct

Discuss ideas, implementations, and evidence respectfully. Welcome people
with different backgrounds and experience. Be precise about uncertainty,
credit others' work, and distinguish criticism of a claim from criticism of
the person making it.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sharing private information without
permission, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not post private
credentials or identifying information as part of a disagreement.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions, or
restrict participation to protect constructive research. Decisions should
be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct where privacy permits.
For sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer through an available private
GitHub channel; do not publish private details to obtain attention. GitHub's
platform reporting tools remain available for abuse.

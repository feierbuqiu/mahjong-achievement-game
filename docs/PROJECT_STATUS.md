# Project status at v1.0.0

**v1.0.0 closes the maintainer's active phase of work on this project.**
It fixes the two English papers, source code, public evidence and reproduction
materials as an immutable release. Version 1 denotes a completed research
delivery, not completion of every mathematical or formal-verification target.

The computational conclusion remains a second-player win from the empty
table. The full computation and reverse replay agree, while sharing rules
and indexing. The end-to-end empty-position and ten-tile Lean theorems remain
`NOT_VERIFIED`. General model proofs, concrete nonempty certificates, compiled
finite checks and synthetic implementation tests retain their separate scopes.

The maintainer will not undertake further end-to-end or full-scale Lean closure
verification and does not plan routine new research runs or progress reports.
Further active follow-up is expected only if substantive new results appear
or a correction, reproducibility problem or other concrete need warrants it.
There is no promised schedule for further research or review.

The repository remains available for issues and signed contributions from
people with the necessary expertise and compute. Useful work includes an
independent argument, discharging the documented formal premises, stronger
data binding, or a measured and verifiable computational improvement.
The frozen v1.0.0 record will remain unchanged; justified subsequent changes
can be recorded on the repository and, when appropriate, in later versions.

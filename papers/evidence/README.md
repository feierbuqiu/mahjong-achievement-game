# Evidence availability and provenance

The primary paper is [the English manuscript](../manuscript.md). This catalogue covers its cited local sources and selected later progress records. A **public copy** is downloadable here; a **retained source** has only its identity and availability recorded. Historical scripts and logs are inspection material, not the supported execution interface. Use [reproduction](../../docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and [Lean replay](../../formal/README.md).

Original-language historical notes and the Chinese manuscript are not uploaded. The mathematical arguments are presented in English in the paper and its three English audit memos. Large tables, SQLite checkpoints, binaries, toolchains and private workstation paths are excluded. Original hashes continue to identify original bytes; public hashes identify the copies actually distributed.
